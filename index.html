The house band

Meet The Purrancers

Eight cats, one house band. They play any style and any part a song asks for, and every one of them sings. Some songs I sing myself; on others the band carries them. The songs are always mine.

Now in 3D as well: the same eight cats, built in code, playing every note of the song on stage in the Sphere. Drag across the sheet to see them both ways.
