05The artistIn the studio
The artist

A HeART strong life is all I wish to live

Yoeshi is a multidisciplinary artist working across music, writing, and visual compositions. His work explores how people connect, create meaning, and navigate change, especially at the intersection of human emotion and emerging technology.

Yoeshi’s art is meant to be experienced, not consumed. It invites listeners and viewers to slow down, engage, and find personal meaning within the work.

01

Music

The core motivator for everything I do is my longing to write songs about the most important things to all of us and sing them for those who want to listen.

Read on

For as long as I can remember, I have believed that music has a greater power over humans than any other artform, and this is why I feel a responsibility to contribute intentional, heartstrong, and (hopefully) timeless songs to the story of humanity by any means necessary while I am here in this lifetime.

02

Digital art

The core motivator for my visual art is storytelling. My visual practice began as a way to create imagery that could coexist with my music and convey the same emotion and narrative.

Read on

Each release is documented through smart contracts and NFTs, forming interconnected story worlds built through art, technology, and community.

03

Communication

Communication has always been central to how I move through the world. Composing words became a way to understand people, emotion, and meaning.

Read on

Alongside writing, I host a weekly X Space with Tezos Commons, where artists on Tezos are interviewed, and their art is spotlighted.
